            Leave project?

            Your task and your stake are removed from the project. No money has moved yet, because stakes are only deposited when the smart contract is created.

            To rejoin, you'll need a new invitation.

            Delete project?

            This removes the project for every member, along with its tasks, stakes and invitations. It can't be undone.

            No money has moved yet, because stakes are only deposited when the smart contract is created.

            How evaluation works

            At the deadline, an LLM scores each member's code contribution from 0–10, with feedback and commit evidence.

            The backend settles the pool and members withdraw their allocation.

            Non-code work is not measured.
